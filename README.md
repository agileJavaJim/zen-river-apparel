# Zen River — Peace, Love, & a Touch of Menace

Zen River is an apparel and lifestyle brand for the mindfully irreverent: graphic apparel, home decor, drinkware, and yoga accessories that blend funny mindfulness quotes, gothic artwork, and spiritual graphics.

This repository holds the brand's standalone landing page — a single static file, no build step, ready for GitHub Pages.

## Live site

https://agilejavajim.github.io/zen-river-apparel/

## What's on the page

- **Hero** — Peace, Love, & a Touch of Menace, over the dark sacred-geometry artwork.
- **Core Values** — Mindfulness, Humor, and Self-Expression.
- **Visionary Essentials** — Visionary Hawk, Owl, and Elephant beach towels, the Visionary Mandala Desk Mat, and Visionary Hawk and Owl yoga mats, each linked to its product page.
- **Irreverent Graphic Apparel** — Peace, Love & a Touch of Menace, Inner Peace. Outer Chaos., Quietly Judging Your Vibe, Inhale Good / Exhale Bullshit, Namaste Away From Me, and the Gothic Wings & Roses Hoodie.
- **Home Decor & Drinkware** — Raven & Skull Canvas Tote, Raven & Skull Drinkware, Raven & Full Moon Pillow, and the Gothic Raven Desk Setup.
- **Our Ethos** — Mindfulness with an edge: authenticity over fake smiles.

Just one file: `index.html`. Open it in a browser to preview it, or publish it with GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main`, folder `/ (root)`).
